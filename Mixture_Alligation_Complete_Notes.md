# 📐 Master Formula Sheet & Core Concepts

## 1.1 The Golden Law of Mixture Removal & Residual Ratio Invariance

> **Fundamental Rule:** When any quantity of a homogeneous mixture is drawn out, the components (e.g., Milk & Water, Alcohol & Water) are removed in the **EXACT SAME RATIO** as they exist in the mixture.  
> Consequently, the ratio of components in the **REMAINING MIXTURE** remains **100% IDENTICAL** to the initial ratio.

$$\text{Initial Ratio } (M : W) \xrightarrow{-\,k\text{ Litres Drawn Out}} \text{Remaining Ratio } (M : W) \quad \mathbf{[\text{Ratio Unchanged!}]}$$

* **Example:** If a $100\text{L}$ mixture has Milk : Water $= 3 : 2$, and $20\text{L}$ is removed:
  * In the $20\text{L}$ removed: $\text{Milk} = \frac{3}{5} \times 20 = 12\text{L}$, $\text{Water} = \frac{2}{5} \times 20 = 8\text{L}$.
  * In the remaining $80\text{L}$: $\text{Milk} = 60 - 12 = 48\text{L}$, $\text{Water} = 40 - 8 = 32\text{L}$.
  * $\text{Ratio} = 48 : 32 = 3 : 2$ (Remains intact!).

---

## 1.2 Single Component Addition vs Dual Component Variation

### Case 1: When ONLY ONE component is added (e.g., Water added)
* The **OTHER COMPONENT (Milk)** quantity remains **ABSOLUTELY CONSTANT**.
* **Kaushik Sir's Shortcut:** Make the ratio parts of the constant component equal in both the remaining and final ratios. Then, the difference in the variable component ratio directly equals the added quantity in units.

$$\text{Remaining } (M : W) = 5 : 7 \xrightarrow{+\,18\text{L Water}} \text{Final } (M : W) = 1 : 2 \equiv 5 : 10$$
$$\Delta \text{Water} = 10 - 7 = 3\text{ units} = 18\text{L} \implies 1\text{ unit} = 6\text{L}$$

### Case 2: When BOTH components are added / modified
* Use the **Ratio Constant Method** or setup a simple 1-variable algebraic equation:
$$\frac{M_{\text{rem}} + M_{\text{added}}}{W_{\text{rem}} + W_{\text{added}}} = \frac{M_{\text{final}}}{W_{\text{final}}}$$

---

## 1.3 Equalizing Quantities (Same Ratio Addition Property)

If a mixture has components in ratio $a : b$, and quantities are added in the **EXACT SAME RATIO** $(ka : kb)$, then the resulting mixture **STILL HAS THE EXACT SAME RATIO** $a : b$.

$$\frac{ax + ka}{bx + kb} = \frac{a(x + k)}{b(x + k)} = \frac{a}{b}$$

* **Direct Takeaway (Exam Hack):** If the initial ratio is $3:4$ and the final ratio is $3:4$, the quantities added *must* also be in the ratio $3:4$.

---

## 1.4 Successive Dilution & Multiple Replacement Master Formula

When a vessel initially contains $C_i$ quantity of a **pure liquid** (or initial component), and $y$ quantity is drawn out and replaced with water/another liquid, and this operation is performed $n$ times:

$$\Large \mathbf{C_f = C_i \times \left(1 - \frac{y}{V}\right)^n}$$

Where:
* $C_i = \text{Initial quantity of pure liquid}$
* $C_f = \text{Final remaining quantity of that liquid}$
* $y = \text{Quantity drawn out and replaced in each cycle}$
* $V = \text{Total capacity / volume of vessel (kept constant)}$
* $n = \text{Number of repeated operations}$

$$\mathbf{\frac{\text{Final Pure Liquid}}{\text{Total Capacity}} = \left(1 - \frac{y}{V}\right)^n}$$

---

## 1.5 Fractional Tracking Method (The "Never Added" Component Rule)

When different quantities ($y_1, y_2, y_3 \dots$) are removed at different steps:

$$\Large \mathbf{\text{Final Quantity} = \text{Initial Quantity} \times \left(1 - \frac{y_1}{V_1}\right) \times \left(1 - \frac{y_2}{V_2}\right) \times \left(1 - \frac{y_3}{V_3}\right)}$$

* **Kaushik Sir's Rule:** Always track the component that is **NEVER ADDED BACK**.
* Once the final quantity of that component is found:
$$\text{Final Added Component (Water)} = \text{Total Volume} - \text{Final Untouched Component (Milk)}$$

---

## 1.6 The Rule of Alligation (Cross Method Framework)

Alligation is a modified visual form of finding weighted averages.

$$\begin{array}{ccc}
\text{\textbf{Cheaper / Type 1}} & & \text{\textbf{Dearer / Type 2}} \\
c & & d \\
& \diagdown \quad \diagup & \\
& \text{\textbf{Mean Value }}(m) & \\
& \diagup \quad \diagdown & \\
(d - m) & : & (m - c) \\
\text{\textbf{Quantity 1 }}(q_1) & : & \text{\textbf{Quantity 2 }}(q_2)
\end{array}$$

$$\Large \mathbf{\frac{q_1}{q_2} = \frac{d - m}{m - c}}$$

---

## 1.7 Weighted Average Derivation & Alligation Equivalence

$$\text{Mean Value }(m) = \frac{q_1 c + q_2 d}{q_1 + q_2}$$
$$m(q_1 + q_2) = q_1 c + q_2 d \implies q_1(m - c) = q_2(d - m) \implies \mathbf{\frac{q_1}{q_2} = \frac{d - m}{m - c}}$$

---

## 1.8 Direct vs Indirect Alligation

1. **Direct Alligation (Finding Quantities Ratio):**
   * Given: Individual rates/concentrations $c, d$ and desired mean $m$.
   * Result: Ratio of quantities $q_1 : q_2 = (d - m) : (m - c)$.

2. **Indirect / Reverse Alligation (Finding Mean Value $m$ or Missing Concentration):**
   * Total Range $= |d - c|$.
   * Divide the total range in the inverse ratio of quantities $(q_2 : q_1)$.
   * Mean Value $= c + \left(\frac{q_2}{q_1 + q_2} \times (d - c)\right)$.

---

## 1.9 Applications Across Quant

| Arithmetic Domain | Value 1 ($c$) | Value 2 ($d$) | Mean Value ($m$) | Ratio Obtained ($q_1 : q_2$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mixtures** | Concentration 1 | Concentration 2 | Mixed Concentration | **Volume / Weight of Solutions** |

## <a id="question-01"></a>📌 Question 01
**Source:** Slide 12 & 13

### 📝 Problem Statement (Bilingual)
**English:**  
The ratio of milk to water in a mixture is $5 : 7$. If $40$ Litres of mixture is taken out and then $18$ Litres of water is added to the mixture, the ratio of milk to water in the mixture becomes $1 : 2$. Find the initial quantity of the mixture.

**हिन्दी:**  
एक मिश्रण में दूध और पानी का अनुपात $5 : 7$ है। अगर मिश्रण में से $40$ लीटर मिश्रण निकाल लिया जाए और फिर उसमें $18$ लीटर पानी मिला दिया जाए, तो मिश्रण में दूध और पानी का अनुपात $1 : 2$ हो जाता है। मिश्रण की शुरुआती मात्रा ज्ञात कीजिए।

---

### 🔘 Options
* **a)** $120$ liters
* **b)** $112$ liters
* **c)** $100$ liters
* **d)** $90$ liters
* **e)** $80$ liters

**Correct Answer:** **b) 112 liters**

---

### 🎯 Kaushik Sir's Approach (Ratio Invariance & Equalization Method)
1. **Ratio Invariance on Removal:**
   * Initial Ratio $(\text{Milk} : \text{Water}) = 5 : 7$.
   * When $40\text{L}$ mixture is taken out, the ratio of the **Remaining Mixture** is STILL $5 : 7$.
2. **Water Addition Step:**
   * $+18\text{L}$ water is added, resulting in Final Ratio $= 1 : 2$.
   * Since **ONLY WATER** was added, the quantity of **MILK** remains unchanged!
3. **Equalize Milk Units:**
   * Remaining Ratio: $\text{Milk} : \text{Water} = 5 : 7$
   * Final Ratio: $\text{Milk} : \text{Water} = 1 \times 5 : 2 \times 5 = 5 : 10$
4. **Compare Water Units:**
   * Increase in water $= 10 - 7 = 3\text{ units}$.
   * Given increase $= 18\text{ Litres} \implies 3\text{ units} = 18\text{L} \implies \mathbf{1\text{ unit} = 6\text{L}}$.
5. **Calculate Quantities:**
   * Total Remaining Mixture (before adding water) $= 5 + 7 = 12\text{ units} = 12 \times 6 = 72\text{ Litres}$.
   * Initial Quantity of Mixture $= \text{Remaining} + \text{Removed} = 72 + 40 = \mathbf{112\text{ Litres}}$.

---

### 📘 Basic / Algebraic Approach
* Let initial total mixture $= 12x$ (where Milk $= 5x$, Water $= 7x$).
* $40\text{L}$ removed:
  * Milk removed $= \frac{5}{12} \times 40 = \frac{50}{3}\text{L}$
  * Water removed $= \frac{7}{12} \times 40 = \frac{70}{3}\text{L}$
* $18\text{L}$ water added:
  $$\frac{5x - \frac{50}{3}}{7x - \frac{70}{3} + 18} = \frac{1}{2}$$
  $$\frac{15x - 50}{21x - 70 + 54} = \frac{1}{2} \implies \frac{15x - 50}{21x - 16} = \frac{1}{2}$$
  $$2(15x - 50) = 21x - 16 \implies 30x - 100 = 21x - 16$$
  $$9x = 84 \implies x = \frac{84}{9} = \frac{28}{3}$$
* Initial Total Mixture $= 12x = 12 \times \frac{28}{3} = 4 \times 28 = \mathbf{112\text{ Litres}}$.

---

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Revision Tips
* Always analyze from the **remaining state** after removal. This avoids messy fractions and reduces the problem to basic ratio scaling!

## <a id="question-02"></a>📌 Question 02
**Source:** Slide 14

### 📝 Problem Statement (Bilingual)
**English:**  
In a $240$-litre mixture, water constitutes $37.5\%$ and the remaining portion is milk. If $80$ litres of the mixture is removed and then $X$ litres of milk is added, the ratio between milk and water becomes $11 : 5$. Find the value of $X$.

**हिन्दी:**  
$240$ लीटर के मिश्रण में $37.5\%$ पानी है और बाकी हिस्सा दूध है। अगर मिश्रण में से $80$ लीटर निकाल लिया जाए और फिर $X$ लीटर दूध मिलाया जाए, तो दूध और पानी का अनुपात $11 : 5$ हो जाता है। $X$ का मान ज्ञात कीजिए।

---

### 🔘 Options
* **a)** $48$ liters
* **b)** $40$ liters
* **c)** $32$ liters
* **d)** $30$ liters
* **e)** $24$ liters

**Correct Answer:** **c) 32 liters**

---

### 🎯 Kaushik Sir's Approach (Percentage to Ratio & Constant Component)
1. **Convert Percentage to Ratio:**
   * Water $= 37.5\% = \frac{3}{8} \implies \text{Milk} = \frac{5}{8}$.
   * Initial Ratio $(\text{Milk} : \text{Water}) = 5 : 3$.
2. **Deduce Remaining Volume:**
   * Total Initial $= 240\text{L}$.
   * Removed $= 80\text{L} \implies \text{Remaining Volume} = 240 - 80 = 160\text{ Litres}$.
3. **Analyze Remaining Components:**
   * In $160\text{L}$ with ratio $5 : 3$ ($8\text{ units} = 160\text{L} \implies 1\text{ unit} = 20\text{L}$):
   * $\text{Milk} = 5 \times 20 = 100\text{L}$
   * $\text{Water} = 3 \times 20 = 60\text{L}$
4. **Milk Addition Step:**
   * $+X\text{L}$ milk is added $\implies \text{Water remains constant at } 60\text{L}$.
   * New Ratio: $\text{Milk} : \text{Water} = 11 : 5$.
   * $5\text{ units of Water} = 60\text{L} \implies 1\text{ unit} = 12\text{L}$.
   * New Milk $= 11\text{ units} = 11 \times 12 = 132\text{ Litres}$.
5. **Find $X$:**
   $$X = \text{New Milk} - \text{Remaining Milk} = 132 - 100 = \mathbf{32\text{ Litres}}$$

---

### 📘 Basic / Algebraic Approach
* In remaining $160\text{L}$: $\text{Milk} = 100\text{L}$, $\text{Water} = 60\text{L}$.
$$\frac{100 + X}{60} = \frac{11}{5} \implies 5(100 + X) = 660 \implies 500 + 5X = 660 \implies 5X = 160 \implies X = \mathbf{32\text{ Litres}}$$

---

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Revision Tips
* $37.5\% = 3/8$. Remember the common bank fraction conversions: $12.5\% = 1/8$, $37.5\% = 3/8$, $62.5\% = 5/8$, $87.5\% = 7/8$.

## <a id="question-03"></a>📌 Question 03
**Source:** Slide 15

### 📝 Problem Statement (Bilingual)
**English:**  
A vessel contains $80\%$ milk, and the remaining quantity is water. $90$ litres of the mixture is removed from the vessel, after which $x$ litres of milk is added. The ratio of milk to water then becomes $9 : 2$. Find the value of $x$, if the difference between the quantities of milk and water immediately after removing $90$ litres of the mixture is $36$ litres.

**हिन्दी:**  
एक बर्तन में $80\%$ दूध है और बाकी पानी है। बर्तन से $90$ लीटर मिश्रण निकाला जाता है, जिसके बाद $x$ लीटर दूध मिलाया जाता है। इसके बाद दूध और पानी का अनुपात $9 : 2$ हो जाता है। $x$ का मान ज्ञात कीजिए, यदि $90$ लीटर मिश्रण निकालने के ठीक बाद दूध और पानी की मात्राओं के बीच का अंतर $36$ लीटर है।

---

### 🔘 Options
* **a)** $6$
* **b)** $8$
* **c)** $12$
* **d)** $18$
* **e)** None of these

**Correct Answer:** **a) 6**

---

### 🎯 Kaushik Sir's Approach (Post-Removal Difference Analysis)
1. **Initial Ratio Breakdown:**
   * $\text{Milk} = 80\%$, $\text{Water} = 20\% \implies \text{Ratio } (\text{Milk} : \text{Water}) = 4 : 1$.
2. **Post-Removal State:**
   * After removing $90\text{L}$, ratio is still $4 : 1$.
   * Difference in ratio $= 4 - 1 = 3\text{ units}$.
   * Given post-removal difference $= 36\text{ Litres}$.
   * $3\text{ units} = 36\text{L} \implies \mathbf{1\text{ unit} = 12\text{L}}$.
   * Quantities immediately after removal:
     * $\text{Milk} = 4 \times 12 = 48\text{ Litres}$
     * $\text{Water} = 1 \times 12 = 12\text{ Litres}$
3. **Milk Addition Step:**
   * $x$ litres of milk is added $\implies \text{Water remains } 12\text{L}$.
   * Final Ratio: $\text{Milk} : \text{Water} = 9 : 2$.
   * $2\text{ units of Water} = 12\text{L} \implies 1\text{ unit} = 6\text{L}$.
   * Final Milk $= 9 \times 6 = 54\text{ Litres}$.
4. **Calculate $x$:**
   $$x = 54 - 48 = \mathbf{6\text{ Litres}}$$

---

### 📘 Basic / Algebraic Approach
$$\frac{48 + x}{12} = \frac{9}{2} \implies 2(48 + x) = 108 \implies 96 + 2x = 108 \implies 2x = 12 \implies x = \mathbf{6}$$

---

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Revision Tips
* Notice that the initial total capacity wasn't even needed! The $36\text{L}$ difference directly gives the remaining quantities in one step.

## <a id="question-04"></a>📌 Question 04
**Source:** Slide 16

### 📝 Problem Statement (Bilingual)
**English:**  
A vessel contains $60$ litres of milk, while the remaining quantity is water, which constitutes one-fourth of the total mixture. If $(x)$ litres of the mixture is removed and replaced with the same quantity of water, the ratio of milk to water becomes $3 : 2$. Find the value of $(x)$.

**हिन्दी:**  
एक बर्तन में $60$ लीटर दूध है, जबकि बाकी मात्रा पानी है, जो कुल मिश्रण का एक-चौथाई हिस्सा है। अगर मिश्रण में से $(x)$ लीटर निकालकर उतनी ही मात्रा में पानी मिला दिया जाए, तो दूध और पानी का अनुपात $3 : 2$ हो जाता है। $(x)$ का मान ज्ञात कीजिए।

---

### 🔘 Options
* **a)** $12$
* **b)** $16$
* **c)** $18$
* **d)** $20$
* **e)** None of these

**Correct Answer:** **b) 16**

---

### 🎯 Kaushik Sir's Approach (Constant Total Capacity Replacement)
1. **Determine Total Initial Volume:**
   * Water $= \frac{1}{4} \implies \text{Milk} = \frac{3}{4} = 60\text{ Litres}$.
   * Total Mixture $= 60 \times \frac{4}{3} = 80\text{ Litres}$.
   * $\text{Initial Water} = 80 - 60 = 20\text{ Litres}$.
   * $\text{Ratio } (\text{Milk} : \text{Water}) = 3 : 1$.
2. **Final State Analysis:**
   * Since $x$ litres is removed and replaced with the same quantity of water, **Total Volume remains 80 Litres**.
   * Final Ratio: $\text{Milk} : \text{Water} = 3 : 2$ ($5\text{ units} = 80\text{L} \implies 1\text{ unit} = 16\text{L}$).
   * Final Milk $= 3 \times 16 = 48\text{ Litres}$.
3. **Track Milk Reduction:**
   * Milk decreased from $60\text{L}$ to $48\text{L} \implies \text{Milk Removed} = 60 - 48 = 12\text{ Litres}$.
   * Since milk is $\frac{3}{4}$ of the mixture:
     $$\frac{3}{4} \times x = 12 \implies x = 12 \times \frac{4}{3} = \mathbf{16\text{ Litres}}$$

---

### 📘 Basic / Algebraic Approach
* Formula: $\text{Final Milk} = \text{Initial Milk} \left(1 - \frac{x}{\text{Total}}\right)$
$$48 = 60 \left(1 - \frac{x}{80}\right) \implies \frac{48}{60} = 1 - \frac{x}{80} \implies \frac{4}{5} = 1 - \frac{x}{80} \implies \frac{x}{80} = \frac{1}{5} \implies x = \mathbf{16}$$

---

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Revision Tips
* Whenever replacement is done with equal volume, tracking the fraction of milk left gives $x$ in 5 seconds via $\frac{\text{Final}}{\text{Initial}} = 1 - \frac{x}{V}$.

A $468$-litre mixture contains only alcohol and water in the ratio $9 : 4$, respectively. If $X$ litres of the mixture is taken out, the quantity of alcohol remaining in the vessel becomes equal to the initial quantity of water in the mixture. Find the value of $X$.

$468$ लीटर के मिश्रण में अल्कोहल और पानी का अनुपात क्रमशः $9 : 4$ है। यदि मिश्रण में से $X$ लीटर निकाल लिया जाए, तो बर्तन में बचे अल्कोहल की मात्रा, मिश्रण में पानी की शुरुआती मात्रा के बराबर हो जाती है। $X$ का मान ज्ञात कीजिए।

---

### 🔘 Options
* **a)** $130$
* **b)** $156$
* **c)** $195$
* **d)** $234$
* **e)** $260$

**Correct Answer:** **e) 260**

---

### 🎯 Kaushik Sir's Approach (Direct Ratio Tracking)
1. **Calculate Initial Quantities:**
   * Total Volume $= 468\text{ Litres}$, Ratio $(\text{Alcohol} : \text{Water}) = 9 : 4$.
   * Total units $= 9 + 4 = 13\text{ units} = 468\text{L} \implies \mathbf{1\text{ unit} = 36\text{L}}$.
   * $\text{Initial Alcohol} = 9 \times 36 = 324\text{ Litres}$.

### 📝 Problem Statement (Bilingual)

### 🔘 Options
* **a)** $26.66\%$
* **b)** $33.33\%$
* **c)** $37.5\%$
* **d)** $40\%$
* **e)** None of these

---

### 🎯 Kaushik Sir's Approach (Smart Unit Assumption Method)
1. **Choose a Convenient Initial Total Volume:**
   * Final ratio sum $= 7 + 8 = 15\text{ units}$.
   * Final volume is $25\% = \frac{5}{4}$ of Initial Volume.
   * Let $\text{Initial Volume} = 12\text{ units} \implies \text{Final Volume} = 12 \times \frac{5}{4} = 15\text{ units}$.
2. **Breakdown Initial Components:**
   * In $12\text{ units}$ with ratio $2 : 1$:
   * $\text{Initial Milk} = 8\text{ units}$, $\text{Initial Water} = 4\text{ units}$.
3. **Breakdown Final Components:**
   * In $15\text{ units}$ with ratio $7 : 8$:
   * $\text{Final Milk} = 7\text{ units}$, $\text{Final Water} = 8\text{ units}$.
4. **Calculate Removed Volume '$z$':**
   * Milk reduced from $8\text{ units}$ to $7\text{ units} \implies \text{Milk Removed} = 1\text{ unit}$.
   * Since milk is $\frac{2}{3}$ of mixture removed:
     $$\frac{2}{3} \times z = 1\text{ unit} \implies z = 1.5\text{ units}$$
5. **Calculate Added Volume '$x$':**
   * Volume after removal $= \text{Initial} - z = 12 - 1.5 = 10.5\text{ units}$.
   * $+x$ water is added to reach Final Volume of $15\text{ units}$:
     $$x = 15 - 10.5 = 4.5\text{ units}$$
6. **Calculate Percentage:**
   $$\text{Percentage} = \frac{z}{x} \times 100 = \frac{1.5}{4.5} \times 100 = \frac{1}{3} \times 100 = \mathbf{33.33\%}$$

---

### 📘 Basic / Algebraic Approach
* Let Initial Volume $= V$. $\text{Milk} = \frac{2}{3}V$.
* After removing $z$, $\text{Milk remaining} = \frac{2}{3}(V - z)$.
* Final Volume $= 1.25V \implies \text{Final Milk} = \frac{7}{15}(1.25V) = \frac{7}{15} \times \frac{5}{4} V = \frac{7}{12} V$.
* Equating Milk:

* Volume balance: $(V - z) + x = 1.25V \implies \frac{7}{8}V + x = \frac{5}{4}V \implies x = \frac{3}{8}V$.
* $\frac{z}{x} = \frac{1/8}{3/8} = \frac{1}{3} = \mathbf{33.33\%}$.

$(x + 30)$ लीटर के मिश्रण में केवल अल्कोहल और पानी है, जिसमें अल्कोहल की मात्रा पानी से $20$ लीटर कम है। यदि मिश्रण में से $(x - 18)$ लीटर निकाल लिया जाए और उसकी जगह $12$ लीटर अल्कोहल और $15$ लीटर पानी मिला दिया जाए, तो अल्कोहल और पानी की मात्रा का अनुपात $2 : 3$ हो जाता है। "$x$" का मान ज्ञात कीजिए।

### 🔘 Options
* **a)** $90$ liters
* **b)** $80$ liters
* **c)** $60$ liters
* **d)** $50$ liters
* **e)** None of these

**Correct Answer:** **d) 50 liters**

---

### 🎯 Kaushik Sir's Approach (Ratio Difference Invariance Method)
1. **Understand Initial and Remaining Ratio:**
   * In the original mixture, $\text{Water} - \text{Alcohol} = 20\text{L}$.
   * Since removal does NOT change the ratio, let remaining ratio be $A : W$.
2. **Setup from Post-Removal State:**
   * Let remaining quantities be $A = 3k$ and $W = 5k$ (or setup linear ratio):
   * Since $12\text{L}$ alcohol and $15\text{L}$ water are added to give $2 : 3$:
   $$\frac{A_{\text{rem}} + 12}{W_{\text{rem}} + 15} = \frac{2}{3}$$
   * Since the initial ratio has difference $20\text{L}$ in $(x+30)\text{L}$, remaining mixture has same ratio $3 : 5$ ($30\text{L} : 50\text{L}$ base).
   * Let $A_{\text{rem}} = 3k, W_{\text{rem}} = 5k$:
   $$\frac{3k + 12}{5k + 15} = \frac{2}{3} \implies 3(3k + 12) = 2(5k + 15)$$
   $$9k + 36 = 10k + 30 \implies \mathbf{k = 6}$$
3. **Calculate Volumes:**
   * $\text{Remaining Mixture Volume} = 3k + 5k = 8k = 8 \times 6 = 48\text{ Litres}$.
   * Original Total Volume had $\text{Alcohol} = 30\text{L}, \text{Water} = 50\text{L} \implies \text{Total} = 80\text{ Litres}$.
   * Given $\text{Original Volume} = x + 30$:
     $$x + 30 = 80 \implies \mathbf{x = 50}$$
4. **Verification:**
   * Removed $= x - 18 = 50 - 18 = 32\text{ Litres}$.
   * Remaining $= 80 - 32 = 48\text{ Litres}$ (Matches $8k = 48$ perfectly!).

---

### 📘 Basic / Algebraic Approach
* In initial mixture: $W - A = 20$, $W + A = x + 30 \implies W = \frac{x + 50}{2}, A = \frac{x + 10}{2}$.
* Removed fraction $= \frac{x - 18}{x + 30}$.
* Remaining volume $= (x + 30) - (x - 18) = 48\text{ Litres}$.
* Ratio in $48\text{L}$: $A = 18\text{L}, W = 30\text{L}$ (since ratio is $3 : 5$).
* After addition: $\frac{18 + 12}{30 + 15} = \frac{30}{45} = \frac{2}{3}$ (Valid).
* Original volume $= \frac{48}{1 - \frac{x - 18}{x + 30}} = 80 \implies x + 30 = 80 \implies x = \mathbf{50}$.

   * Ratio of $(\text{Added Mass from A}) : (\text{Initial Mass of B}) = 8 : 7$.
   * Given mass added from A $= 64\text{ kg}$.
   * $8\text{ units} = 64\text{ kg} \implies \mathbf{1\text{ unit} = 8\text{ kg}}$.
4. **Determine Initial Quantity of Vessel B:**
   $$\text{Initial Quantity of B} = 7 \times 8 = \mathbf{56\text{ kg}}$$

---

### 📘 Basic / Algebraic Approach
* Let initial quantity of vessel B $= B\text{ kg}$.
* Sugar from A $= 80\% \text{ of } 64 = 51.2\text{ kg}$.
* Sugar initially in B $= 20\% \text{ of } B = 0.2B\text{ kg}$.
* Total mixture in B $= 64 + B\text{ kg}$.
$$\frac{51.2 + 0.2B}{64 + B} = \frac{13}{25} = 0.52$$
$$51.2 + 0.2B = 0.52(64 + B) = 33.28 + 0.52B$$
$$51.2 - 33.28 = 0.52B - 0.2B \implies 17.92 = 0.32B \implies B = \frac{17.92}{0.32} = \mathbf{56\text{ kg}}$$

---

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Revision Tips
* Notice how Alligation turns complicated decimal algebra ($17.92 / 0.32$) into simple whole number ratio matching: $8\text{ units} = 64 \implies 7\text{ units} = 56$!

A vessel contains $80$ litres of a mixture of milk and water. If $25\%$ of the milk and $10\%$ of the water are removed separately, then $84\%$ of the initial mixture remains in the vessel. Find the initial amount of water in the initial mixture.

एक बर्तन में दूध और पानी का $80$ लीटर मिश्रण है। अगर दूध का $25\%$ और पानी का $10\%$ अलग-अलग निकाल दिया जाए, तो बर्तन में शुरुआती मिश्रण का $84\%$ हिस्सा बचता है। शुरुआती मिश्रण में पानी की शुरुआती मात्रा पता करें।

### 🔘 Options
* **a)** $16$
* **b)** $24$
* **c)** $32$
* **d)** $48$
* **e)** None of these

**Correct Answer:** **d) 48**

---

### 🎯 Kaushik Sir's Approach (Alligation on Percentage Removed)
1. **Determine Overall Removal Percentage:**
   * Mixture remaining $= 84\% \implies \text{Total Mixture Removed} = 100\% - 84\% = \mathbf{16\%}$.
2. **Setup Alligation Cross on Removed Rates:**
   $$\begin{array}{ccc}
   \text{\textbf{Milk Removed}} & & \text{\textbf{Water Removed}} \\
   25\% & & 10\% \\
   & \diagdown \quad \diagup & \\
   & 16\% & \\
   & \diagup \quad \diagdown & \\
   (16 - 10) = 6 & : & (25 - 16) = 9 \\
   \mathbf{2} & : & \mathbf{3}
   \end{array}$$

   * $\text{Ratio } (\text{Initial Milk} : \text{Initial Water}) = 2 : 3$.
   * Total Units $= 2 + 3 = 5\text{ units} = 80\text{ Litres} \implies \mathbf{1\text{ unit} = 16\text{ Litres}}$.

---

* Let Milk $= M$, Water $= W$, $M + W = 80$.
* Removed: $0.25M + 0.10W = 0.16 \times 80 = 12.8$.
* Multiply by 10: $2.5M + W = 128$.
* Subtract $M + W = 80$:
  $$1.5M = 48 \implies M = \frac{48}{1.5} = 32\text{ Litres}$$
* $W = 80 - 32 = \mathbf{48\text{ Litres}}$.

---

* Whenever individual percentage changes and overall percentage change are given, Alligation gives the base quantity ratio directly in 5 seconds!

## <a id="question-30"></a>📌 Question 30
**Source:** Slide 48

### 📝 Problem Statement (Bilingual)
**English:**  
In mixture P, the ratio of milk to water is $3 : 5$, while in mixture Q, the ratio of milk to water is $5 : 3$. If mixtures P and Q be mixed in the ratio $1 : 5$, then the final mixture contains milk and water in what ratio?

**हिन्दी:**  
मिश्रण P में दूध और पानी का अनुपात $3 : 5$ है, जबकि मिश्रण Q में दूध और पानी का अनुपात $5 : 3$ है। यदि मिश्रण P और Q को $1 : 5$ के अनुपात में मिलाया जाए, तो अंतिम मिश्रण में दूध और पानी का अनुपात क्या होगा?

---

### 🔘 Options
* **a)** $12 : 7$
* **b)** $7 : 12$
* **c)** $5 : 7$
* **d)** $7 : 5$
* **e)** None of these

**Correct Answer:** **d) 7 : 5**

---

### 🎯 Kaushik Sir's Approach (Equal-Sum Ratio Scaling Method)
1. **Check Base Unit Sums:**
   * Mixture P: $\text{Milk} : \text{Water} = 3 : 5 \implies \text{Sum} = 3 + 5 = 8\text{ units}$.
   * Mixture Q: $\text{Milk} : \text{Water} = 5 : 3 \implies \text{Sum} = 5 + 3 = 8\text{ units}$.
   * **Great! Both sums are ALREADY EQUAL to 8 units.**
2. **Scale by Mixing Ratio ($1 : 5$):**
   * Take $1$ portion of P (Scale by $1$):
     * $\text{Milk} = 3 \times 1 = 3$
     * $\text{Water} = 5 \times 1 = 5$
   * Take $5$ portions of Q (Scale by $5$):
     * $\text{Milk} = 5 \times 5 = 25$
     * $\text{Water} = 3 \times 5 = 15$
3. **Sum Combined Components:**
   * $\text{Total Milk} = 3 + 25 = 28$
   * $\text{Total Water} = 5 + 15 = 20$
4. **Determine Final Ratio:**
   $$\text{Final Ratio } (\text{Milk} : \text{Water}) = 28 : 20 = \mathbf{7 : 5}$$

---

### 📘 Basic / Algebraic Approach
* In 1 unit of P: $\text{Milk} = \frac{3}{8}$, $\text{Water} = \frac{5}{8}$.
* In 5 units of Q: $\text{Milk} = 5 \times \frac{5}{8} = \frac{25}{8}$, $\text{Water} = 5 \times \frac{3}{8} = \frac{15}{8}$.
* $\text{Total Milk} = \frac{3 + 25}{8} = \frac{28}{8}$.
* $\text{Total Water} = \frac{5 + 15}{8} = \frac{20}{8}$.
* $\text{Ratio} = \frac{28/8}{20/8} = \frac{28}{20} = \mathbf{7 : 5}$.

---

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Revision Tips
* Always equalize base sums before multiplying by the mixing ratio! Since both were already 8, direct multiplication by 1 and 5 gave the answer in 10 seconds.

## <a id="question-31"></a>📌 Question 31
**Source:** Slide 49

### 📝 Problem Statement (Bilingual)
**English:**  
Two mixtures containing milk and water are combined in the ratio $4 : 7$. The resulting mixture contains $58\%$ milk. If the first mixture contains $86\%$ milk, find the percentage of milk in the second mixture.

**हिन्दी:**  
दूध और पानी वाले दो मिश्रणों को $4 : 7$ के अनुपात में मिलाया जाता है। बनने वाले मिश्रण में $58\%$ दूध होता है। अगर पहले मिश्रण में $86\%$ दूध है, तो दूसरे मिश्रण में दूध का प्रतिशत पता करें।

---

### 🔘 Options
* **a)** $74\%$
* **b)** $52\%$
* **c)** $42\%$
* **d)** $32\%$
* **e)** None of these

**Correct Answer:** **c) 42%**

---

### 🎯 Kaushik Sir's Approach (Reverse Alligation Cross)
1. **Setup Alligation Structure:**
   * Mix 1 Concentration $= 86\%$
   * Mix 2 Concentration $= x\%$
   * Mean Concentration $= 58\%$
   * Ratio of Quantities $= 4 : 7$
2. **Calculate Given Difference:**
   $$\text{Difference on Mix 1 side} = 86\% - 58\% = 28\%$$
3. **Map to Ratio Units:**
   * This $28\%$ corresponds to the $7$ parts of Mix 2!
   $$7\text{ units} = 28\% \implies \mathbf{1\text{ unit} = 4\%}$$
4. **Find Missing Difference:**
   * The other side has $4$ parts:
   $$4\text{ units} = 4 \times 4\% = 16\%$$
5. **Determine $x$:**
   * Since $86\% > 58\%$ (Mean), the second concentration $x$ MUST be lower than $58\%$:
   $$x = 58\% - 16\% = \mathbf{42\%}$$

---

### 📘 Basic / Algebraic Approach
$$\frac{86 \times 4 + x \times 7}{4 + 7} = 58$$
$$\frac{344 + 7x}{11} = 58 \implies 344 + 7x = 638 \implies 7x = 638 - 344 = 294 \implies x = \frac{294}{7} = \mathbf{42\%}$$

---

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Revision Tips
* Remember the Mean property: $c < m < d$. Since $86 > 58$, the missing value is $58 - 16 = 42\%$, not $58 + 16 = 74\%$!

## <a id="question-32"></a>📌 Question 32 (Home-Work)
**Source:** Slide 50

### 📝 Problem Statement (Bilingual)
**English:**  
A container holds $240$ litres of a milk-and-water mixture in which water constitutes $30\%$ of the total quantity. A milkman sells $40$ litres of this mixture to a customer and then adds $50$ litres of water to the remaining mixture. Find the percentage of milk in the final mixture.

**हिन्दी:**  
एक कंटेनर में दूध और पानी का $240$ लीटर मिश्रण है, जिसमें पानी की मात्रा कुल मात्रा का $30\%$ है। एक दूधवाला इस मिश्रण में से $40$ लीटर एक ग्राहक को बेचता है और फिर बचे हुए मिश्रण में $50$ लीटर पानी मिला देता है। आखिर में बने मिश्रण में दूध का प्रतिशत पता करें।

---

### 🔘 Options
* **a)** $40\%$
* **b)** $44\%$
* **c)** $56\%$
* **d)** $60\%$
* **e)** None of these

**Correct Answer:** **c) 56%**

---

### 🎯 Kaushik Sir's Approach (Dual Method: Component Tracking & Alligation)
#### Method 1: Remaining Milk Volume Tracking (Fastest)
1. **Analyze Remaining Mixture:**
   * Initial $= 240\text{L}$, Water $= 30\% \implies \text{Milk} = 70\%$.
   * Sold $= 40\text{L} \implies \text{Remaining Volume} = 240 - 40 = 200\text{ Litres}$.
   * In remaining $200\text{L}$, Milk is STILL $70\%$:
   $$\text{Remaining Milk} = 70\% \text{ of } 200 = 140\text{ Litres}$$
2. **Add $50\text{L}$ Water:**
   * New Total Volume $= 200 + 50 = 250\text{ Litres}$.
   * Since only water was added, Milk remains $140\text{ Litres}$.
3. **Calculate Final Milk Percentage:**
   $$\text{Final Milk } \% = \frac{140}{250} \times 100 = \frac{14}{25} \times 100 = 14 \times 4 = \mathbf{56\%}$$

#### Method 2: Alligation on Milk Concentration
1. **Mixture Portion ($200\text{L}$):** Milk $= 70\%$.
2. **Added Water Portion ($50\text{L}$):** Milk $= 0\%$.
3. **Ratio of Quantities:** $200 : 50 = 4 : 1$.
4. **Range Splitting:** Total range $= 70\% - 0\% = 70\%$.
   * Split in ratio $1 : 4 \implies \text{Parts} = 14\% : 56\%$.
   * $\text{Mean Milk } \% = 70\% - 14\% = \mathbf{56\%}$ (or $0\% + 56\% = 56\%$).

---

### 📘 Basic / Algebraic Approach
$$\text{Final Milk } \% = \frac{0.70 \times (240 - 40)}{(240 - 40) + 50} \times 100 = \frac{0.70 \times 200}{250} \times 100 = \frac{140}{250} \times 100 = \mathbf{56\%}$$

---

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Revision Tips
* Dividing by $250$ and multiplying by $100$ is equivalent to multiplying by $4$ and shifting the decimal by 1 place: $\frac{140}{250} \times 100 = 14 \times 4 = 56\%$!

# 📊 Quick Formula & Speed-Hacks Cheat-Sheet

| Topic / Scenario | Master Shortcut / Formula | Key Observation / Exam Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mixture Removal Invariance** | $\text{Ratio}_{\text{initial}} = \text{Ratio}_{\text{remaining}}$ | Components are always removed in identical proportions. |
| **Single Component Addition** | Equalize the unchanged component's ratio units | $\Delta \text{variable units} = \text{Added quantity}$. |
| **Equal Ratio Addition** | $\frac{ax + ka}{bx + kb} = \frac{a}{b}$ | If initial and final ratios match, added parts share same ratio. |
| **Equal Volume Replacement ($n$ times)** | $\frac{C_f}{V} = \left(1 - \frac{y}{V}\right)^n$ | For $n=3$, take cube root; for $n=2$, take square root. |
| **Unequal Multiple Dilutions** | $C_f = C_i \prod \left(1 - \frac{y_k}{V}\right)$ | Always track the component that is **never added back**. |
| **Alligation Rule (Cross)** | $\frac{q_1}{q_2} = \frac{d - m}{m - c}$ | Higher value $(d)$ minus Mean $(m)$ vs Mean $(m)$ minus Lower $(c)$. |
| **Weighted Average Mean** | $m = \frac{q_1 c + q_2 d}{q_1 + q_2}$ | Useful when mixing parts in direct numeric ratios. |
| **Range Splitting Trick** | $\text{Unit Value} = \frac{\|d - c\|}{q_1 + q_2}$ | Add $(\text{Unit Value} \times q_2)$ to the lower concentration $c$. |
| **Two Mixture Consolidation** | Scale by equal base sums first | Make ratio sums equal before multiplying by mixing weights. |
| **Percentage Removed Mix** | Alligation on individual $\%_{\text{removed}}$ | Overall $\%_{\text{removed}}$ becomes the central mean $m$. |
